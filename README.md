## 📖 Longer projects

**MSc thesis**

- **[msc-thesis-hla-mutation](https://github.com/helenalm04/msc-thesis-hla-mutation)**:
  *Mutational Path Sampling on HLA Presentation Landscapes* (Imperial College London,
  2026, supervised by Dr Barbara Bravi and Yinfei Yang) — how a peptide can
  mutate out of presentation by one HLA class I allele and into presentation by
  another. I built an MCMC sampler over mutational paths, combining Gibbs and
  Metropolis–Hastings moves with an annealed burn-in on restricted Boltzmann machine
  energy landscapes, and validated it against two exact dynamic-programming solvers
  using split-R̂ and effective sample size as convergence diagnostics. The
  question was whether information-theoretic distances between two fitted models
  predict how hard the transition between them is; they do not.

**Machine learning**

- **[eval-measurement](https://github.com/helenalm04/eval-measurement)**: how far
  you can trust an automatic score for LLM answers, and how many human ratings you
  actually need. I built a Catalan benchmark and a rule-based scoring harness, rated
  model answers myself as a native speaker, and combined the two with
  prediction-powered inference and a Bayesian model with my own Gibbs sampler.

- **[vae-gpt-pytorch](https://github.com/helenalm04/vae-gpt-pytorch)**: a β-VAE
  with convolutional variants and sliced Wasserstein evaluation, and a decoder-only
  GPT transformer, both built from scratch in PyTorch.

**Probability and statistics**

- **[probabilistic-modelling-from-scratch](https://github.com/helenalm04/probabilistic-modelling-from-scratch)**:
  a Bernoulli mixture model for binarised MNIST trained with EM, including
  log-sum-exp stable responsibilities and sampling from the fitted model, and
  Gaussian Process regression implemented from scratch comparing squared exponential
  and Laplace covariance kernels.
